One model, every question you can ask it.

  • Analysis

    More than 25 structural properties: boundedness, liveness, conflicts, siphons and traps, with the verdict and the reason for each.

  • Invariants

    P- and T-invariants from the incidence matrix, so you can prove conservation laws instead of guessing them.

  • Graphs

    Reachability graph, or a coverability tree for unbounded nets, with ω shown where the marking grows without limit.

  • CTL

    Write properties in a readable formula language and get a verdict with a witness or a counterexample path.

  • Token Game

    Fire enabled transitions one at a time and watch the marking change, exactly like the model above.

  • Reductions

    Stubborn sets, symmetries and structural reductions keep the state space small enough to finish.

Opens .pnt files from INA and Mercury scripts.

From a file to an answer in three steps.

  1. 1

    Open your net

    Load a .pnt file or paste a Mercury script. SPECTRA validates the model before anything runs.

  2. 2

    Ask a question

    Pick a structural check, compute invariants, build the state space, or write a CTL formula.

  3. 3

    Read the evidence

    Inspect graphs, markings and paths, and replay any trace in the token game.
